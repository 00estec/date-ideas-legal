3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide to us

When you create an account. You can sign up with an email and password, or with Google or Apple sign-in. If you use Google or Apple, we receive basic profile information (such as your name and email) from that provider, according to their settings and your choices. We do not require you to verify your email address before using the App.

During onboarding and in your profile. To personalise the App, we collect: your first name; your date of birth (used to confirm you are 18 or over, see Section 14, and for nothing else); your gender (required when you set up your account; collected to personalise the date ideas we suggest, and not used for anything else); your relationship status; your city and its country; and your preferences (date types, vibes, budget, and travel distance). You may also add a profile photo and an anniversary date.

Content you create in the App. We store the content you create, including: saved date ideas and bucketlist folders; planned dates and itineraries; timeline entries (the dates you've been on, with optional titles, dates, locations, notes, "favourite moments," reactions, vibe tags, and photos); reviews (reactions, recommendations, vibe tags, text, and photos); and swipes/likes in partner-matching sessions.

Date Finder answers. When you use the Date Finder, the answers you give (for example: out or at home, the kind of date, the occasion, indoors or outdoors, the season and your budget) are sent to us together with your city, and we use them only to rank date ideas and show you the best matches. We do not store your answers: they are used to build that one list of results and are not saved to your account or kept on our servers.

Photos. When you upload photos (to your profile, timeline entries, or reviews), we store them with our storage provider, in private, access-controlled storage. Photos you attach to a review are not shown with the review: only you can see them, plus your linked partner if you share the memory the review adds to your timeline. Your profile photo can be seen by your linked partner, and is shown with any review you write (see Section 8).

Feedback and support. When you submit feedback or a bug report, we collect your message and, optionally, screenshots and basic device information (operating system, device model, and app version) to help us diagnose the issue. When you submit a date-idea suggestion, we collect the details you provide.

Safety reports. When you report content or block another user, we record the report (including the reason and any description you add) and the block, so our team can review and act on it.

3.2 Information we collect automatically

Usage data. As you use the App, we record activity such as the date ideas you view, your searches, your quiz answers, your swipes, and when you tap an affiliate/booking link. We use this to power features (like "Recently Viewed" and "not seen before" recommendations), to measure how popular an idea is, and to understand and improve the Service.

Device and technical data. We collect your device model, operating system version, app version, and time zone (we store your time zone on your account, to send notifications at the right local time). If you enable push notifications, we store a device push token. Our infrastructure providers also process your IP address and connection information as part of delivering the Service and protecting it from abuse.

Anonymous (guest) usage. If you use the App without an account, we create an anonymous session so that public content loads securely. Activity from that session (for example, ideas you view) is associated with an anonymous identifier. If you later create an account, that history carries over to your new account.

6. Location data

Finding dates near you is a core purpose of the App, so location deserves its own section.

  • It's optional. The App does not require location access to function. If you decline, you can still use the App: we show you at-home ideas, long-distance ideas, and experiences that work anywhere, rather than places near you.
  • Foreground only, with your permission. If you tap to share your location, the App asks your device for permission and reads your precise location only while you are using the App (in the foreground). We do not track your location in the background, and we do not continuously monitor your movements.
  • What we do with it. We use your device location only to work out which city you are in. We store that city on your profile, along with the name of the place your device reports (used to personalise what the App says to you), its country (kept on your record only; it is not shown in the App and does not change what you see), and the exact coordinates your device shared.
  • Typing your city instead. If you would rather not share your location, you can type the name of your city. The letters you type are sent to Google Places, which suggests matching cities; when you pick one, we receive that city's name, country and the coordinates of its centre, store them on your profile, and use them in the same way as a device location: to work out which city you are in. We do not send your device location for this.
  • How to turn it off. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device's settings. The App then stops reading your device's location, but the city and coordinates already saved on your profile stay there, and the App keeps using them to choose your ideas. If you have an account, you can change your city: go to Profile → View profile → City → Update, find your city again or type another one, and tap Save changes. A typed city replaces the coordinates from your device with the centre of the city you pick. The App has no option to remove your city altogether: email us at hello@dateideasnearme.co and we will remove it.

When you view a map of a venue, the App draws it with Google Maps, on iPhone and Android, and the map's coordinates are sent to Google to draw the map; Directions opens Google Maps. Google's Maps software collects device and diagnostic information (such as your device model, operating system, crash and performance reports, IP address and an identifier used only by Google Maps) and how you move the map (panning and zooming), which Google uses for its own purposes under Google's policies (see Section 11).

7. Partner and couple linking

The App lets you link with a partner using an invite QR code or a pairing code. When you link:

  • We create a shared "group" and record both members' membership.
  • Certain content becomes shared with your linked partner: for example, the shared bucketlist, shared timeline entries, and the "you both liked" matches. Your partner will be able to see shared content you add.
  • Individual swipe choices are never revealed to your partner: only ideas you both liked are shown as matches.
  • Content you keep private (for example, a private save) is not shared with your partner.

When you unlink or a partner deletes their account, shared access is revoked. Previously shared content stays associated with the old group and becomes inaccessible to both people (it may be restored if you reconnect). If a partner deletes their account, the content they created and shared is removed.

What the other person is told. When someone accepts your invite, we send you a push notification saying they accepted, with their first name (if your phone allows notifications from the App; this message is sent whatever you choose for Partner activity in the App's notification settings). When you link as a couple, we also set both your relationship status and your partner's to "In a relationship"; you can change yours afterwards under Profile → View profile. Unlinking is silent: we do not notify the other person, and we do not notify a partner when you delete your account. This is to help protect users in sensitive situations. You can also block another user.

8. Reviews, photos, and who can see them

Reviews are shown only to people signed in to an account, not to people browsing as a guest. If you block someone, or they block you, neither of you sees the other's reviews.

  • What other signed-in users see on your review: your first name, your profile photo, your reaction, the text you wrote, and when you posted it. From the time you write your first review, other signed-in users (apart from anyone you have blocked or who has blocked you) can view your profile photo.
  • Details stored with your review but not shown on it: whether you recommend the idea, the vibe tags you picked, the day you went, and your favourite moment. The App does not show these to other people, but they are stored with the review in a way that any signed-in user could read, including people you have blocked, so please treat them as visible too.
  • Photos you attach to a review are not shown with the review and are not public. Only you can see them, plus your linked partner if you share the memory the review adds to your timeline.

Please don't include information in a review that you wouldn't want other people to see. Photos can contain more than you expect (for example, backgrounds that reveal a location). Your timeline entries are not part of reviews: they are private to you (and shared with your linked partner if you share them).

If you believe content violates our Terms, you can report it using the in-app "Report" option. See our Terms of Service for how moderation works.

9. Affiliate links and bookings

Some date ideas include booking or purchase links. Certain of these are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you book or buy through them, at no extra cost to you. When you tap a booking or affiliate link, we record the click (including which idea and, if applicable, your group context) to attribute bookings, measure interest, and prevent click fraud.

When you follow a booking/affiliate link, you leave our Service and interact with a third-party website or app. Their handling of your information is governed by their privacy policies, not ours (see Section 17).

10. Analytics, diagnostics, and push notifications

Push notifications. If you enable push notifications, we store a device push token and use Expo's push notification service, which delivers messages through Apple Push Notification service (APNs) on iOS and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) on Android. We use notifications for things like date reminders, partner activity, matches, and the Weekly Date Drop. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Diagnostics and analytics. To keep the App reliable and to understand how it is used, the App uses these two services:

  • Sentry: crash and error reports, so we can find and fix bugs. Sentry receives details of the error (including the web addresses the App was contacting when it happened), basic device details (such as the device model, operating system and app version), a random identifier for your installation of the App, and the IP address of your connection. We have set Sentry not to attach cookies or request contents, and we do not tell Sentry who you are, but its reports can still identify your account: the web addresses it records can contain your account ID, and when something goes wrong on our servers while handling your account (for example, a failed account deletion), the report names your account ID.
  • PostHog: product analytics, so we can understand which features are used and improve the App. The App sends PostHog a short list of named events (for example, that the App was opened, an idea was saved or a booking link was tapped), with basic device information. PostHog's automatic capture of taps and screens is turned off, and we never send PostHog your name, your email address, text you type or your precise location.

We do not use advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA) to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and provide the controls required by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and applicable law.

12. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, and then delete or anonymise it.

  • While your account is active, we keep your account and content so the Service works as expected.
  • When you delete your account (see Section 13), we schedule your data for permanent deletion after a 14-day grace period, during which you can cancel. After that period, we permanently delete your profile, saves, timeline entries, reviews, planned dates, uploaded photos, and group memberships from our live systems. Copies in our database provider's backups are overwritten on that provider's schedule.
  • Guest/anonymous session data. If you use the App without an account, the answers you give (such as your city) stay on your phone until you sign up, when they are uploaded to your account. The ideas you view and the booking links you tap while a guest are recorded against your guest session on our servers, and carry over to your account when you sign up. You can ask us to delete guest data at any time by emailing hello@dateideasnearme.co.
  • Certain records may be retained, in minimal or anonymised form, for longer where we have a legal or safety reason to do so: for example, content/safety reports, and records needed to prevent fraud or meet legal obligations.
  • Diagnostic data (crash and error reports) can include your account ID (see Section 10), but it is not removed when you delete your account. It is kept for as long as our crash-reporting provider retains it under our plan with them.
  • Analytics data (product-usage events) is not tied to your account, so it is not removed when you delete your account. It is kept for as long as our analytics provider retains it under our plan with them.

14. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adults aged 18 and over, and is not directed to anyone under 18. We ask for your date of birth just after you create your sign-in (an email and password, or Google or Apple), while you set up your account. If the date shows you are under 18, the App does not let you go any further: you cannot finish setting up your account or use any feature that needs one, and the date you entered is not saved to your account. The sign-in you created (your email address and sign-in details), and anything recorded while you browsed as a guest, stay on our servers until they are deleted: the App does not delete them automatically. Once a date of birth is saved, it cannot be changed, to prevent circumventing this age gate.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at hello@dateideasnearme.co and we will delete it.

15. Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect your information, including: encryption of data in transit (HTTPS); access-controlled and, for user photos, private storage; database-level access controls so you can only access your own data (and shared data within your group, and other people's reviews as described in Section 8); secure storage of authentication tokens on your device; and least-privilege access to our systems.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Please help protect your account by using a strong, unique password and keeping your device secure.
